The Real-life Thesis
The app wins when people leave the app.

Found is not trying to become another feed. The goal is to turn online intent into offline repetition: small groups, simple plans, and enough structure for real friendships to form in the real world.

1

Create small groups around compatibility, energy, life stage, humor, availability, and shared context.
2

Build local density

How it works

Your AI social concierge makes meeting IRL feel easier.

Real friendship is built through repeated, low-stakes, shared experiences. Found is designed to reduce the friction that keeps those moments from happening.

1
Tell Found who you are
A short conversational onboarding learns your personality, energy, interests, schedule, and the kinds of people you usually click with.
2
Found suggests your group
Found uses compatibility signals to create small groups with shared context, complementary personalities, and a real chance of clicking offline.
3
Make the first plan
Found can suggest the venue, timing, format, and icebreaker context so the first hangout feels simple instead of awkward.
4
Keep the cadence
After each meetup, Found nudges the group toward the next low-stakes plan - helping acquaintances build the repetition that friendship needs.
5
The group takes over
As the group gets comfortable, Found becomes less central. The goal is not more app time. The goal is real people making real plans.

Why now

The old friendship infrastructure is fraying. Found is a new layer for real life.

Work, school, neighborhoods, and nightlife do not automatically create durable friendships for many adults anymore. Found gives that process structure.

AI can coordinate the messy parts
Matching, reminders, preferences, context, and follow-up are exactly the kinds of repeated coordination tasks AI can make easier.
Remote and hybrid life changed habits
Fewer casual daily encounters means fewer accidental friendships. Found is built for intentional, real-world connection.
Meeting through software feels normal
People already use apps to meet dates, colleagues, communities, and activity partners. Friendship is the next obvious use case.
Low-pressure plans matter
The best gatherings do not need to be expensive or elaborate. Found can steer groups toward simple, repeatable ways to meet.

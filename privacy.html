Privacy Policy

Found is an early-stage product concept from Bonzoli Technology Group. This policy explains what information may be collected as the site and waitlist evolve.

Information we may collect

If you join the waitlist, contact us, or request investor information, we may collect information such as your name, email address, city, message content, and any information you choose to provide.

How we may use it

  • To respond to you and manage early access interest.
  • To understand which launch cities and user groups are showing interest.
  • To improve the Found concept, messaging, and product experience.
  • To send updates if you have asked to receive them.

Cookies and analytics

This static version of the site may not use analytics yet. If analytics, pixels, or cookies are added later, they should be used to understand site performance, traffic sources, and waitlist conversion.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers such as hosting, analytics, forms, email, or database tools to operate the site and early access process.

Your choices

You may ask to update or remove your information by emailing hello@imfound.app.
